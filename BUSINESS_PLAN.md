## 1. Executive Summary

Renewal Loop is a modular apparel company that treats clothing as a long-term repairable platform. Our garments feature a patented **Snap-and-Secure** seam system that lets users replace high-wear components (elbows, knees, collars, cuffs, zippers, pockets) in seconds — extending garment life from 1-2 years to 5+ years and reducing textile waste by up to 70%.

We generate revenue through three streams: one-time garment sales ($89-$149), a recurring **Refurbish Plan** subscription ($15/month), and individual panel sales ($12-$35). A B2B channel offers custom-branded panels for uniform and workwear markets.

**Key metrics:**
- Gross margin: ~60% on garments, ~75% on panels
- Customer LTV: $340+ over 3 years (garment + subscription + panels)
- CAC target: $35 (D2C organic + referral)
- Payback period: 4 months
- Break-even: Month 14 at ~2,800 active subscribers

### 2.2 Revenue Stream Detail

#### Garment Sales (Primary)
- Launch with 3 SKUs: Jacket ($129), Vest ($89), Pants ($119)
- Each garment ships with standard black panels included
- Customer chooses garment size; panels auto-sized to match
- Color/upgraded panels sold separately as accessories

#### Refurbish Plan (Recurring — Strategic Priority)
- $15/month subscription
- Includes: 2 replacement panels per quarter (customer's choice of type/size/color)
- Free shipping on all panel orders
- 1 free repair credit per year (if snap hardware fails, we mail a Refurbish Kit)
- Early access to new panel colors and limited editions
- 10% discount on additional panel purchases
- **Rationale:** Converts one-time buyers into recurring revenue. The subscription is the moat — it makes Renewal Loop the default maintenance provider for the garment's entire 5+ year life.

#### Panel Sales (On-Demand)
- Individual panels priced $12-$35 based on complexity
- Elbow: $15 | Knee: $18 | Collar: $12 | Cuff: $12 | Zipper: $25 | Pocket: $14
- Sold to both subscribers (10% discount) and non-subscribers (pay-per-panel)
- Enables impulse color swaps and style refreshes

#### B2B Custom (Contract)
- Custom-branded panels for uniform/workwear/trades customers
- Corporate colors, logo embroidery, bulk pricing
- Minimum order: 50 panels or 20 garments
- Pricing: negotiated per contract, typically $60-$90 per garment with bulk panel resupply at $8-$20/panel
- Target customers: trades companies, outdoor guides, restaurant groups, facilities teams

## 4. Go-to-Market Strategy

### 4.1 Phase 1: D2C Online First (Months 7-12)

- **Channel:** Direct e-commerce (Medusa.js storefront on renewalloop.com)
- **Acquisition:** 
  - Organic content (sustainability + durability storytelling on TikTok, Instagram, YouTube)
  - Paid social (Meta + TikTok ads targeting sustainability-conscious consumers, outdoor enthusiasts, trades workers)
  - Influencer seeding (micro-influencers in sustainable fashion, workwear, outdoor)
  - SEO (long-tail: "repairable jacket," "replaceable zipper clothing," "sustainable workwear")
- **Conversion:** Pre-order campaign on landing page (Month 7-9), full store launch Month 10
- **Retention driver:** Refurbish Plan subscription pitched at checkout with first month free

### 4.2 Phase 2: Pop-Up Markets (Months 10-18)

- **Channel:** In-person pop-up booths at farmers' markets, craft fairs, outdoor festivals, trade shows
- **Purpose:** Tactile product demo — let people feel the snap mechanism and swap panels live
- **Target events:** Sustainability expos, outdoor gear festivals, maker faires, trade industry events
- **Conversion:** QR code → online store with event-specific discount code
- **Cost:** ~$500-$1,500/event including booth, travel, samples
- **Expected ROI:** 15-25 garment sales per event + email signups + brand awareness

### 4.3 Phase 3: Retail Partnerships (Months 18-36)

- **Channel:** Selective wholesale to independent outdoor retailers, sustainable fashion boutiques, workwear specialists
- **Target partners:** REI (long-term), Patagonia-aligned independent retailers, local sustainable boutiques, trade supply stores
- **Margin concession:** Wholesale at 55% of retail (retailer keeps 45%)
- **Differentiation:** In-store panel demo station — customers swap panels in-store
- **Entry strategy:** Start with 5-10 independent retailers in pilot markets (Portland, Denver, Brooklyn), expand based on sell-through
- **Subscription attach:** Retail customers can subscribe to Refurbish Plan via in-store QR code; retailer gets $5/mo referral for first year

### 4.4 B2B Channel (Ongoing from Month 12)

- **Channel:** Direct outreach to trades companies, outdoor guide services, restaurant groups, facilities management teams
- **Value prop:** Branded uniforms that last 5+ years with $15/month maintenance vs. $60+ replacement every 6 months
- **Pricing:** Negotiated contract, typically $60-$90/garment + $8-$20/panel resupply
- **Target:** 10 B2B accounts in Year 1, 50+ by Year 2
- **Margins:** Lower (~50%) but high volume and predictable recurring revenue

## 6. Competitive Landscape

| Competitor | Model | Weakness vs. Renewal Loop |
|---|---|---|
| Patagonia (Ironclad Guarantee) | Repair service, not modular | Requires sending garment in; weeks-long turnaround; no user-serviceable parts |
| Nudie Jeans (free repairs) | In-store repair, not replaceable | Geographic limitation; repair not replacement; still requires visit |
| Fast fashion (H&M, Zara) | Disposable, low cost | No durability, no repairability, high waste |
| Premium workwear (Carhartt, Duluth) | Durable but not modular | When a component fails, whole garment is done; no user-serviceable panels |
| **Renewal Loop** | **Modular, user-serviceable, subscription** | **Panels replaceable in seconds at home; subscription auto-ships replacements; 5+ year garment life** |

**Our moat:** The Snap-and-Secure mechanism + the Refurbish Plan subscription. The hardware is patentable; the subscription creates recurring revenue and lock-in (users accumulate panels that only fit Renewal Loop garments).

## 7. Risk Analysis

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Manufacturing delays | Medium | High | Dual-source manufacturers; 6-week buffer in timeline |
| Snap mechanism failure in field | Low | Critical | 50-hour wear test before production; load-tested to 2x rated force; warranty + Refurbish Kit |
| Low subscription attach rate | Medium | High | First-month-free offer at checkout; default opt-in; clear value messaging ($15/mo vs $119 replacement) |
| Competitor copies mechanism | Low (patent pending) | Medium | Provisional patent Month 3; design patent Month 4; trade dress protection |
| Fabric supply disruption | Low | Medium | Dual-source rPET fabric mills; 3-month buffer stock at DC |
| Customer returns / fit issues | Medium | Low | Detailed size guide + adjustable channel forgiving 1 size delta; free first exchange |
